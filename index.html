Honest, fairly priced apps for Jira Cloud

Every app here runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, is priced for the people who actually use it, and matches or beats the incumbent it replaces on features.

AppWhat it does
Honest Timesheets for JiraWorklog grid, timers, approvals, team view and reports, bucketed in each author's own timezone.
Pivot Reports for JiraPivot tables and charts over any JQL, with drill-down, CSV and dashboard gadgets.
Tickbox Checklists for JiraRequired items, auto-applied templates, board progress, JQL fields and workflow gates.
Mailpost for JiraEmail an issue from Jira with templates, recipients from the issue and a send log.
Agile Gadgets for JiraTwenty dashboard gadgets — burndown, velocity, cumulative flow, cycle time, time in status, aging WIP, throughput, pivots, KPI gauges, a team board and a TV wallboard — rebuilt from issue history.
Stencil Issue Templates for JiraIssue templates with whole epic → story → sub-task trees, placeholders and prompted variables, applied wherever work items are actually created.
Git Links for JiraCommits, branches and pull requests on the issue for GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket and Azure DevOps.
