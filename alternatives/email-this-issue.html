A cheaper alternative to Email This Issue for Jira Cloud

Mailpost for Jira emails a work item from Jira, with templates, recipients taken from the issue, automatic emails on events, scheduled JQL digests and replies that come back as comments. It costs US$0.60 per user per month for 11–100 users, against US$2.00 on Email This Issue's Standard edition.

This page sets out where the two differ, including where Email This Issue is the better choice. Email This Issue is a mature, well-rated app with around 5,700 installs; if you rely on the things listed under where Mailpost is weaker, keep it.

Where Mailpost is weaker

  • No mailbox connectors. Mailpost cannot read an existing Exchange, Microsoft 365, Gmail, IMAP or POP3 mailbox, and it has no S/MIME. Outbound mail to people without a Jira account, real file attachments and incoming mail all need a Postmark account of your own. If you need to read a mailbox you already have, Email This Issue does that and Mailpost does not.
  • Incoming mail adds a comment or creates an issue, and nothing more. It cannot set fields or run a transition. The sender of an email cannot be verified, so we have chosen not to let an email change an issue's state.
  • A daily sending limit. To stop the app being used for bulk mail, each site can send to 200 recipients a day by default (50 during an evaluation), counted across manual sends, rules and digests. Support can raise it for a site that needs more.
  • Coarse scheduling. Forge has no cron, so digests run hourly, daily at a chosen hour or weekly on a chosen day, in UTC.
  • Attachments are capped at 8 MB per file and 10 MB per message on the Postmark path; anything skipped is named to the sender. Without Postmark, emails link to attachments rather than carrying them.
  • You don't get a copy of your own manual send through Jira's notification service, because Jira never emails the person who triggered a notification.
  • Maturity. Email This Issue has around a decade of customers behind it. Mailpost is much newer.

Switching

There is no importer: Velocity templates have to be rewritten in Mailpost's template language, which is usually shorter because it has {{#if}} and {{#each}} but no scripting. Both apps can be installed side by side while you move rules across one project at a time. Mailpost sends through Jira's own notifications out of the box, so you can try it without setting up a mail provider.
