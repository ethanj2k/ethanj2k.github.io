A cheaper alternative to Deep Clone for Jira Cloud

Ditto: Bulk & Deep Clone for Jira copies a work item with everything under it, or every work item a search returns, into the same or another project, including between team-managed and company-managed projects. It costs US$0.55 per user per month for 11–100 users, against US$1.33 for Deep Clone for Jira, and it is free for up to 10 users.

Deep Clone for Jira, by codefortynine, is the category leader with over 11,000 installs and a 4.5-star rating, and it does much more than Ditto. This page sets out where the two differ, including where Ditto is weaker, so you can tell whether you need the extra.

Where Ditto is weaker

Deep Clone does a lot more. Choose it if you need any of these:

  • Project cloning and project templates. Ditto clones work items only, not schemes, workflows, screens, permissions, boards or automation rules.
  • Cloning between Jira sites, or migrating and consolidating sites. Ditto works within one site.
  • Status and history. Ditto's copies start in the target workflow's first status; it does not copy resolution, history or worklogs.
  • Comments attributed properly. Ditto's copied comments appear under the name of whoever ran the clone, which is why they are off by default.
  • Scale. Ditto takes 2,000 work items per job and 10 MB per attachment by default. Larger selections are refused with the number in the message; larger files are listed with their sizes.
  • Automation. Ditto has no presets, post functions or automation actions; every clone is started by a person.
  • Sprint placement may not work on your site. On some sites Atlassian refuses Forge apps access to the Jira Software board API; the clone still runs and the copies go to the backlog.
  • Maturity and assurance. Deep Clone has over 11,000 installs and SOC 2. Ditto is new, with a small install base.

If what you need is to copy work items, with their sub-tasks, children, attachments and links, between projects on the same site, Ditto does that for well under half the price.

Switching

Apart from any presets or post functions you have set up in Deep Clone, there is nothing to migrate. Install Ditto alongside Deep Clone, try it on the clones you do most often, and keep whichever suits you. Ditto appears in the … menu on every work item and in the work item navigator's bulk actions.
