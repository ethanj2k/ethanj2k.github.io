A cheaper alternative to Clone Plus for Jira Cloud

Ditto: Bulk & Deep Clone for Jira copies a work item with everything under it, or every work item a search returns, into the same or another project, including between team-managed and company-managed projects. It costs US$0.55 per user per month for 11–100 users, against US$1.16 for Appfire's Clone Plus for Jira, and it is free for up to 10 users.

Clone Plus is a long-established app with around 1,600 Cloud installs and a 4.4-star rating. This page sets out where the two differ, including where Ditto is weaker.

Where Ditto is weaker

  • No clone templates. Clone Plus lets an admin define clone operations with defaults, conditions and dynamically filled fields. In Ditto each person chooses their options when they clone.
  • Status, resolution, history and worklogs are not copied. Jira does not let an app set status or resolution when it creates a work item.
  • Copied comments are not attributed to their original authors. Jira gives an app no way to do that, so comments are off by default.
  • No project cloning. Ditto clones work items, not a project's schemes, workflows, boards or automation.
  • Sprint placement may not work on your site. On some sites Atlassian refuses Forge apps access to the Jira Software board API; the clone still runs and the copies go to the backlog.
  • English only, and no Cloud Fortified badge.
  • Maturity. Clone Plus has years of customers behind it. Ditto is new, with a small install base.

Common complaints in Clone Plus's reviews, and how Ditto handles them

From the 1–3 star reviews on Clone Plus's Marketplace listing, read in September 2026. Some are old, and the app may have changed since; where its listing now says otherwise, we note it.

What reviewers raisedWhat Ditto does
“It will only work from Team → Team or Company → Company” (2022).This is what Ditto was built around. A team-managed project's fields and issue types belong to that project and share no ids with anything else, so Ditto matches fields by name and type, values by name, and issue types by name, then spelling (“Sub-task” and “Subtask”), then hierarchy level. Anything it can't place is named before the job runs. If two fields on the target share a name, it skips the field and says so rather than guess.
Enabling it for every project is painful for admins.Ditto has no per-project setup at all. Once installed, it is available in every project.
“Doesn't copy attachments at all!” (2020). Clone Plus's listing now says it copies attachments.Ditto copies attachments inside Atlassian's network. Files over the per-file limit are listed with their sizes rather than silently dropped.

Switching

Apart from any clone templates you have defined in Clone Plus, there is nothing to migrate. Install Ditto alongside it, try it on the clones you do most often, and keep whichever suits you. Ditto appears in the … menu on every work item and in the work item navigator's bulk actions.
