Pivot Reports for Jira

Tagline: Pivot tables and charts over any JQL — live, timezone-correct, and a third of the price.

Summary

Pivot Reports turns a JQL query into a pivot table and chart in three clicks: pick a row field, an optional column field, and a measure. Count issues, sum or average story points, time spent, estimates or any number field, or look at cycle time — broken down by status, assignee, priority, type, epic, sprint, version, component, label, any select/user/date custom field, or by created/resolved date per day, week, month, quarter or year. Every cell drills down to the issues behind it.

No import step, no cube, no query language to learn. The app reads Jira live, as you, so what you see is exactly what your permissions allow — and nothing leaves Atlassian's infrastructure (Runs on Atlassian).

Highlights

  • Any two fields, any measure. Rows × columns pivot with row subtotals, column totals and a grand total that always reconcile. Count, sum, average, min, max.
  • Percent views. Show any report as % of total, % of row or % of column.
  • Time buckets that are actually right. Created/resolved/due/custom dates are bucketed in your Jira timezone, not the server's, by day, ISO week, month, quarter or year.
  • Multi-value fields handled honestly. Labels, components, versions and sprints count an issue once per bucket, and the report tells you when the total exceeds the distinct issue count.
  • Native charts. Bar, stacked bar, horizontal bar, line, pie and donut, rendered with Atlassian's own UI Kit components so they match your Jira theme.
  • Drill-down. Click any cell, subtotal or total to list the issues in it.
  • Saved reports. Keep reports private or share them with everyone who can browse the project. Ownership is enforced server-side; project admins can manage shared reports.
  • Dashboard gadget. Put any saved or ad-hoc report on a Jira dashboard.
  • CSV export with RFC 4180 quoting and formula-injection protection.
  • Complete data. The JQL is paged to exhaustion (up to 5,000 issues per report, with a clear warning when a query exceeds that) — never just the first page.
  • Read-only. Scopes: read:jira-work, read:jira-user, storage:app (saved report definitions only — never issue data).

What it is not

Pivot Reports is the deterministic 80% of a BI tool. It does not import data into a cube, has no MDX or custom calculated members, does not track issue history (time-in-status, "as of date"), does not join other data sources, and has no public/external sharing links. If you need those, eazyBI is the right tool — at its price.

Pricing

A 50-person site pays about $150/month for either incumbent and $50/month here; a 10-person team pays nothing.
