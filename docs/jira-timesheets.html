Timesheets for Jira — honest time tracking, priced for actual users

Tagline: See what your team logged against what they were expected to log, straight from native Jira worklogs. No proxy accounts, no per-seat gouging.

Summary

Timesheets turns Jira's own worklogs into a weekly grid per person: hours by issue by day, daily and weekly totals, expected hours from a working pattern, and the variance. It reads worklogs exactly as Jira stores them, so worklogAuthor = currentUser() and every native report keep working.

Highlights

  • Native worklogs, real authors. The app never writes on your behalf and never attributes time to a service account.
  • Expected vs logged, every day. Working patterns and holidays drive an expected-hours row; the variance row shows where the week drifted.
  • Timezone-correct. Work is bucketed in the author's own timezone, so a manager in London sees the same days an engineer in Sydney entered.
  • Runs on Atlassian. No data leaves Atlassian infrastructure. Read-only scopes: read:jira-work, read:jira-user.
  • Clear pricing. Free for up to 10 users. Then a flat, low per-user price — no surprise tiers.

Roadmap (v1.1)

Multi-stage approvals with project-level approvers, bulk approve, and CSV export — the engine is built and tested; the UI ships next.
