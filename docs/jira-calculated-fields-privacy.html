Privacy Policy — Slide Rule: Calculated Fields for Jira

Effective: 9 September 2026

Slide Rule: Calculated Fields for Jira ("the app") is published on the Atlassian Marketplace by Chloe Jane Patricia Johnson, sole trader, ABN 49 732 400 198, Penrith, NSW 2750, Australia ("we", "us"). Contact: ethanjohnson2000@gmail.com.

This policy is specific on purpose: every category of data the app touches is itemised below.

What the app does

The app computes the value of a custom field from a formula a Jira administrator writes — from other fields on the same work item, and optionally from its children, its parent or the work items linked to it — and writes that value back into Jira so it appears on boards, in JQL and in exports. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform and sends data to no server outside Atlassian.

Data the app reads from Jira

When a work item is created or changed, and when a newly saved formula is applied to existing work items, the app reads:

  • the work item being computed: its key, id, project, work item type, parent, and the values and names of every field on it, because a formula may refer to any of them by name;
  • where the formula asks for them, the same for that work item's direct children, its parent, and the work items it is linked to, together with the names of the link types;
  • the list of custom fields on the site, to find the ids of this app's own fields;
  • the account id and display name of the administrator saving a formula (/myself), recorded on the definition as who last changed it;
  • that administrator's permissions (/mypermissions), to check they may administer the site before their change is accepted.

Reads that serve a page a person is looking at are made as that person, with their own Jira permissions. The background recompute runs as the app, because it runs on work items nobody is looking at; see "Data visibility" below for what that means and what the app refuses to do because of it.

Data the app writes to Jira

  • the computed value, into the app's own read-only custom field on the work item. The app can write no other field — a Forge app may only write the values of fields it owns.

Data the app stores

In Forge storage, inside your own Atlassian site:

  • field definitions: the name, type, formula, project and work-item-type scope, on or off, when it was last changed and the account id that changed it;
  • an explanation record per work item: the formula, the value it produced or the reason nothing was written, the timestamp, and the field values the formula read on that work item — which is what the panel on the work item shows;
  • a backfill cursor per definition: an opaque page token and a count.

Nothing else is stored. There is no copy of your work items, and no history beyond the most recent result per work item.

Data visibility

A computed value is an ordinary Jira field value, so anyone who can see the work item can see it, exactly as they can see its other fields. Because the recompute has no reader to check against, the app refuses to compute a rollup when a contributing work item carries an issue-level security level, or when a child is in a different project from its parent — rather than turn a restricted work item into an aggregate everyone can read. The work item says when this has happened, and how many were involved.

Data leaving Atlassian

None. The app declares no permissions.external of any kind: no fetch, no egress, no remote host, no analytics and no runtime AI call. It is not technically capable of contacting any host other than Atlassian's own APIs.

Logging

The app writes operational log lines to Atlassian's Forge logging: work item keys and ids, field ids, counts, and the status of a failed Jira request. It does not log field values, computed values, formulas, or the bodies of Jira's responses.

Retention and deletion

All data is held in Forge storage inside your Atlassian site and is destroyed when the app is uninstalled. Deleting a field definition deletes the definition; the values it has already written remain on your work items until they are cleared, and can be removed by deleting the field itself in Jira's custom field administration.

Your rights

Because the app stores everything inside your own Atlassian site and sends nothing elsewhere, requests about personal data — access, correction, erasure — are answered by your own Atlassian administration. Write to ethanjohnson2000@gmail.com if you need help identifying what the app holds.

GDPR and CCPA

We are a data processor, not a data controller, and a service provider, not a business. We process End-User Data only inside Atlassian apps and services, on your instructions, and we share it with no third party.
